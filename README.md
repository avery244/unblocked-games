# 🎮 Unblocked Games

A modern web-based gaming platform with classic games playable directly in your browser. Perfect for school chromebooks, work breaks, or just some casual gaming fun!

## 🎯 Features

- **6 Classic Games**: Snake, Flappy Bird, Pong, Tetris, Breakout, and 2048
- **No Downloads Required**: Play directly in your browser
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Beautiful UI**: Modern dark theme with gradient effects
- **High Performance**: Optimized for smooth gameplay

## 🕹️ Games Included

### 1. **Snake Game** 🐍
- Classic snake gameplay
- Use arrow keys to move
- Eat food to grow longer
- Avoid walls and yourself

### 2. **Flappy Bird** 🐦
- Simple yet challenging gameplay
- Click or press SPACE to make the bird fly
- Avoid pipes and obstacles
- Score points by passing through gaps

### 3. **Pong** 🏓
- Two-player paddle game
- Player 1: W/S keys
- Player 2: Arrow Up/Down
- First to 5 wins!

### 4. **Tetris** 🧱
- Stack falling blocks
- Complete lines to clear them
- Rotate pieces with Arrow Up
- Difficulty increases with level

### 5. **Breakout** 🎯
- Bounce a ball to break bricks
- Move paddle with arrow keys or mouse
- Break all bricks to win
- Increasing difficulty

### 6. **2048** 2️⃣
- Slide tiles and combine numbers
- Reach the 2048 tile to win
- Use arrow keys to move
- Strategic gameplay

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
3. Select a game from the main menu
4. Click "Play Now" to start
5. Follow the in-game instructions

## 🎮 Controls

### General
- **Arrow Keys**: Move/Control
- **Space/Click**: Action button (varies by game)
- **Mouse**: For games that support mouse control
- **ESC/Click Modal Close**: Exit game

## 📝 Tips & Tricks

- **Snake**: Don't touch yourself! Plan your moves ahead.
- **Flappy Bird**: Time your clicks precisely to navigate through pipes.
- **Pong**: Hit the ball at different angles to confuse your opponent.
- **Tetris**: Think about placement before pieces land.
- **Breakout**: Angle your paddle hits to control the ball direction.
- **2048**: Keep your board organized and plan ahead for merges.
